Secret keypad

1234567890

The whole screen is split into four rows of equal height. The bottom row is all 0. Only where you tap for a digit matters; the tap that starts the video can be anywhere.

Controls

  • Two taps: where you tap to play the video is the first digit. Tap once more while it plays: that's the second digit. The second tap shows the pause symbol for a moment, the way a tap does on any video player, and the video carries on.
  • Play, pause, play (the usual way): tap anywhere to play the video. Tap again to pause it: where you tap is the first digit. Tap to play it again: where you tap is the second digit, and the writing is set. To anyone watching, you just paused the video for a moment.
  • Do the pause before the reveal. If the writing would already be showing when you pause, choose “Start again from the beginning” below, so nobody sees it appear.
  • Digits first (the other way): tap two digits on the still picture, then the next tap anywhere plays.
  • Confirmation: once both digits are in, the file size under the date changes to 4.NN MB, where NN is the out. 4.07 MB means out 07.
  • Swipe down: take back the last tap only, if you hit the wrong place.
  • Swipe left or right: clears the digits and goes back to the start. Use it if you mistype.
  • Hold anywhere (1.5 seconds): opens this screen.

Filming your own video

  • Use a tripod or prop the phone up. The writing stays fixed in the picture, so the camera must not move.
  • Film a surface that can be written on: a whiteboard, paper, a sign, a sticky note. Keep it flat, still and evenly lit.
  • For a reveal, cover the surface at the start and uncover it on camera. When you place the writing, turn on “Hide the writing behind anything darker”.
  • Film in portrait, like the built-in videos; anything outside the phone's screen shape is cropped. 10 to 20 seconds is plenty.

Outs

These belong to the effect chosen at the top. What gets written for each number you tap. Use | to start a new line on the board, e.g. Queen|of Hearts. A blank box writes the number itself. Out 00 leaves the board blank. Picture puts an image on the surface instead of writing, such as a photo of a playing card; a PNG with a see-through background works best. Out 60 has an example.
