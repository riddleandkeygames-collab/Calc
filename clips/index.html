Secret keypad

1234567890

The whole screen is split into four rows of equal height. The bottom row is all 0. Tap two digits, then the next tap anywhere plays the video.

Controls

  • Tap two digits: sets the out (01 to 99). Nothing visible happens, except the confirmation below.
  • Confirmation: the file size under the date changes to 4.NN MB, where NN is the out you tapped. 4.07 MB means out 07.
  • Next tap, anywhere: plays the video. Hand the phone over and let them press play.
  • Swipe left or right: clears the digits and goes back to the start. Use it if you mistype.
  • Hold anywhere (1.5 seconds): opens this screen.

Video

Outs

One per line. Line 01 is out 01, and so on. Use | to start a new line on the board, e.g. Queen|of Hearts. A blank line writes the number itself. Out 00 leaves the board blank.
