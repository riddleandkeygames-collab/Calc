How to perform

  1. Spectators multiply random numbers as normal, e.g. 4821 × 37 =.
  2. The trick is now armed. (If "Trick starts after" is set to two presses, press = again first; the display won't change.) A faint dot appears at the bottom left if they should add, or bottom right if they should subtract.
  3. Have them press + (or −), then tap any number key. The first tap shows the whole secret number. Any further taps are ignored.
  4. Press =. The display shows the date and time.

The secret number is worked out when they press + or −. If the minute might change before the reveal, use the "Add minutes" setting. AC cancels the trick. To open this screen, press and hold the number display for one second.
