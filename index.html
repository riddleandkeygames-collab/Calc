Calculator

What to reveal

Type a word to see its number.

Date and time

Peek: numbers typed

    Secret gestures

    • Hold 1 second: open this screen.
    • Hold the = key for half a second: peek. The last numbers typed appear faintly at the top for 3 seconds. A normal tap on = still works as =.
    • Swipe up: live reveal. If a number is on the display, it becomes the reveal for the next trick and the calculator clears. If the display is clear, the last number a spectator typed is used instead.
    • Swipe left or right: cancel the trick. The calculator then works normally, and the sum still adds up. On the iPhone style, when the trick isn't armed, this deletes the last digit like a real iPhone.

    The phone gives a short buzz when a gesture works, and a double buzz when there's nothing to use.

    How to perform

    1. Spectators do any sum, e.g. 54 × 54 × 54 =.
    2. The trick is now armed. (If "Trick starts after" is set to two presses, press = again first; the display won't change.) A faint dot appears at the bottom left if they should add, or bottom right if they should subtract.
    3. Have them press + (or −), then tap any number key. The first tap shows the whole secret number. Any further taps are ignored.
    4. Press =. The display shows the reveal.

    The secret number is worked out when they press + or −. If the minute might change before the reveal, use "Add minutes". AC or C cancels the trick.
